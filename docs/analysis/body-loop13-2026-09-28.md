## Method

**Metric.** `bl13/common.py` mirrors `eval.rs`: `alignment_words`, `is_math_token`, `is_math_token_char`, `NUMBER_SUFFIXES`, hyphen splitting of math tokens and `drop_math_lines` on the truth. The extracted side of the dump is already `body_only_text`.
- It reproduces `(e, t, m)` of report.json **exactly for all 60 papers**.
- Dev mean is 0.9319.
- Totals: e = 551,745, t = 511,300, m = 495,340.
- Recall is 96.88% and precision 89.78%.
- Extracted-only words: **56,405**. Truth-only words: **15,960**.
- Loop-11 figures (115k / 46k) are not comparable, because the math is now stripped on both sides.

**Runs.** Runs come from difflib, as in loop 11.
- Gaps separated by ≤2 matched words are merged.
- Gaps are split at blank lines and page breaks.
- Counts are unmatched tokens only. difflib over-counts by 261 words per side: E 56,666, T 16,221.
- Runs of ≥6 words are classified by class.
- Gaps of <6 words are reported separately by token kind.

**Oracles.** These are the same as in loop 11:
- `pdftotext` (NFKC)
- a `.tex` 4-gram index with env/argument flags (math tokens are now dropped from source too)
- `tagged_lines` 4-grams
- reference raw-text 4-grams

**Caveat on `tagged_lines`.** It hits the 600-line cap in **36 of 60** dumps, including all six lowest papers. It covers only 55% of non-body tagged lines (31.6k of 57.9k). Over-tag-by-role counts are therefore lower bounds. Truth runs that are in the PDF but missing from the extracted text, and that match no logged tag, are counted as "role unknown".

## 3. Over-tagging per role from `tagged_lines`

Method (`overtag.py`):
- Take each logged line with a non-body role that has ≥5 alignment tokens.
- Search for it as a contiguous token run in the truth.
- Count it as **lost** when most of that truth span is unmatched in the alignment.

36 of 60 dumps are capped. The scaled estimate is computed **per paper and role**: lost words ÷ (logged lines of that role ÷ its `role_counts`), and only for capped papers. The log truncates later pages, so the scaled numbers for capped papers remain approximate.
- Code: per-paper scaling gives about 6,960. That is dominated by 2602.17690, where only 13% of its code lines are logged, and it overshoots.
- The alignment-based count of prompt-box truth loss, **6,278**, is the better estimate.
- Scaled figures for the other roles: figure ~1,060, table ~106, caption ~104, front 126, footnote 21, math 15, algorithm 7.

## 5. Ranked fixes

Gains are dev-mean points. "Upper" is the measured gain from removing the whole class on both sides (`groups.py`); "expected" is a judgement.

| Rank | Fix | File / function | Upper | Expected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
