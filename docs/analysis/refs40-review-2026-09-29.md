## Remaining defect and correction

The new regression reproduced the URL defect with an actual LF before the URL:
the parsed title was `IBM Maximo Application Suite.` rather than
`IBM Maximo Application Suite`. The original fixture used Rust's escaped source
line continuation, so it exercised a space, not a newline.

`title_end` now recognizes whitespace after sentence punctuation rather than
only an ASCII space, and skips that separator without assuming a one-byte
character. Identifier boundaries use the byte ranges of detected DOI, URL and
arXiv identifiers. The parser therefore distinguishes a masked identifier
from ordinary indentation rather than inferring a mask from two spaces.

Coverage includes space, LF, CRLF, tab and nonbreaking space. A negative control
preserves genuine lowercase title continuations across those same separators.
The IEEE regression now asserts the complete title and separate venue, and
four supported quote pairs are tested with multiple commas inside the title.

## PR #57 review corrections

All three subsequent code-review findings were reproduced with failing
regressions before their corrections:

- [Part markers after CRLF or indentation](https://github.com/benpshore/pdftextract/pull/57#discussion_r4131846748):
  the marker pattern now accepts the full whitespace separator, preserving
  `Graph minors.\r\nIV` and the `Part 2` / `Part II` forms.
- [Indentation mistaken for an identifier mask](https://github.com/benpshore/pdftextract/pull/57#discussion_r4131846759):
  title and subtitle decisions receive actual identifier ranges, including
  their offsets within the entry. Plain spaces, tabs, line breaks and
  nonbreaking spaces provide no identifier evidence on their own.
- [Multiline subtitles after question marks](https://github.com/benpshore/pdftextract/pull/57#discussion_r4131846769):
  the nested sentence-boundary check now accepts whitespace after the
  subtitle's punctuation, so its title extends through the subtitle before
  the venue begins.

An additional full-parser regression exposed a panic when a question-mark
title was followed by a nonbreaking space and venue: advancing one byte put
the venue slice inside the UTF-8 character. That cursor now advances one
character. Exact title and venue assertions cover nonbreaking and em spaces,
CRLF and indentation. Identifier-evidence cases cover real DOI, URL and arXiv
ranges after multiline titles, including accented text and a Unicode URL.
