## Method

**Metric.** `bl11/common.py` reproduces the metric exactly for all 60 papers. It has the same tokenizer (`normalize_title`) and the new `is_math_char`/`is_math_heavy`/`drop_math_lines` applied to the truth side, and the exact bit-parallel LCS. `(e, t, m)` equals report.json for every paper.
- Dev mean is 0.8766.
- Totals are e = 649,232, t = 579,995 and m = 534,140.
- Extracted-only words: 115,092 (17.7%). Truth-only words: 45,855 (7.9%).

**Runs.** Runs come from difflib on the full token sequences (`autojunk=False`).
- Gaps separated by ≤2 matched words are merged.
- Each gap is split at blank lines or page breaks, and each block is classified.
- Counts are **unmatched tokens only**, so they tie back to the difflib totals: E 115,597, T 46,360.
- difflib finds 505 fewer matches than the exact LCS, so each side is over-counted by 505 words (0.4%).
- Runs of ≥6 words are classified.
- Gaps of <6 words are counted per token by kind: single character, digits, math glyph, word, absent from the other side, and glued.

**Oracles.**
1. **`pdftotext`** of the same PDF, NFKC-folded so that 𝑥 becomes x. Question: is the truth run printed?
2. **A 4-gram index of all `.tex` files.** Each 4-gram carries a flag. Priority order: `\caption{}`/`\captionof` argument, then `\footnote{}`/`\thanks{}` argument (by brace-matched char range), then front, then table env, then algorithm/code env, then figure env (including `tcolorbox`/`mdframed`), then math env, then prose after `\appendix` or `\bibliography`, then prose.
3. **`tagged_lines` 4-grams.** Were the words over-tagged?
   - **Caveat:** 17 of 60 dumps hit the 600-line cap, including all six lowest papers.
   - The over-tag attribution is therefore a lower bound. Truth runs that are in the PDF but missing from extracted, with no matching tag, are counted as "over-tagged, role unknown".
4. **Truth references' raw text 4-grams.** Used to detect reference leakage.

## 2. The six lowest papers

**2509.04183 (0.649): boxed text inside figure floats.**
- Counts: e = 23,820 against t = 11,684. There are 12,300 E-only words, of which 10,594 are boxed text in `figure*` floats, 994 graphics text and 382 captions. There are only 164 T-only words.
- The appendix consists of `\begin{figure*}\begin{tcolorbox}[title=…]` prompt templates and full example sessions. Truth drops them as figures; regions.rs leaves them untagged because they are real text lines, not graphics.
- One run is 8,031 words: `"MAGneT-generated Session (Example 1)\n\nCounselor: Hello, Leonard. It's nice to meet you. How are you feeling today?\nClient: I've been feeling a bit down lately because I keep thinking that my grandpa is disappointed in me…"`
- Others: `"Client Intake Form\nName: Laura Saunders\nGender: female\nOccupation: Office Job…"` and `"<|start_header_id|>system<|end_header_id|>\nYou are a helpful, respectful, honest, and knowledgeable student studying to become a licensed therapist…"`.
- Removing class 1b alone takes the paper to 0.98.

**2506.23487 (0.690): math on both sides, plus figure text and a leaked reference page.**
- Counts: T-only 3,553, of which 2,463 is math residue and 728 single-letter variables. E-only 4,297: 1,451 math fragments, 969 single-char/glyph fragments, 613 figure text and 415 reference leakage.
- Truth side, detexed proof text: `"gives _x(S)t-ct with c:=2M_+d+2(d)^1/2(1+3p)^1/2, a constant independent of x…"`.
- Extracted side, the PDF's math italic with unmapped glyphs: `"𝜏:\n\n− 𝐼𝑑\n𝑄𝑛 (𝑋)\n� 𝑄𝑖"`, `"𝑋𝑖 = (Age𝑖, Sex𝑖, Prop𝑖"` vs truth `"X_i = (Age_i, Sex_i, Prop_i"`.
- A rotated heat map with its caption shredded: `"tw\nF\no ig.\nw\ns), The\nat\nes obser\nag…\nSOD1\nHSPA8\nPIK3R1…"`.
- **Reference leakage:** the second page of the reference list, `"Hu, W., Pan, T., Kong, D. & Shen, W. (2021). Nonparametric matrix response regression…"`, is emitted between the main text and "Supplementary material / A. Additional simulations".
  - `reference_extents` ended the list at the first page's end.
  - The continuation page starts with an unlabeled author-year entry, which `list_resumes` does not recognize.

**2108.04588 (0.749): inline-math tokenization.**
- Counts: T-only 3,294, of which 1,631 is math residue, 661 math-form spelling and 649 single chars/digits. E-only 2,681: 689 math fragments, 503 spelling, 326 tokens not in truth and 202 glyph tokens.
- The paper is a combinatorial-geometry text whose prose is full of `$…$`.
  - Truth: `"FH: (v_ij)=1mA+(i-1m,j-1m) for (i,j)([n])([m]), (u_1j)=R(-,j-1m] for j=1,…,m+1"`.
  - PDF: `"μ(vij), μ(uij), μ(ūij), μ(zij)"` and `"σA(n1 + n2) ⩽ x1 + x2 ⩽ σA(n1) + σA(n2"` vs truth `"A(n_1+n_2)x_1+x_2_A(n_1)+_A(n_2"`.
- Macros such as `\intrep` become empty in truth. `v_{ij}` gives the tokens `v ij` in truth and `vij` in the PDF.
- A title-page footnote survives in extracted: `"To appear in The Electronic Journal of Combinatorics. A short version of this paper was presented at the 39th International Symposium…"`.
- There are no U+FFFD any more (the loop-10 TeX encodings work).
- Even with every class removed, the paper only reaches 0.90 in variant (b), because the rest is scattered one-token math.

**2601.09974 (0.765): text tables untagged.**
- Counts: e = 14,950 against t = 9,582. E-only 5,562, of which 4,044 are table cells, 465 figure text and 397 captions. T-only 194.
- The tables contain generated-text examples as paragraph cells (`tabularx`/`p{}` columns), which regions.rs does not recognize as a table.
  - An 824-word run: `"Generate a detailed review for a product 'Lenox Expressions from the Heart Teacher Ornament' with\nthe following description: 'Lenox offers a truly legendary collection of ornaments…"`.
  - A 779-word run: `"Generate an abstract for the title "An effective teaching-learning-based optimization algorithm for\nthe flexible job-shop scheduling problem with fuzzy processing time." In this paper…"`.
- Its `tagged_lines` hits the 600 cap (452 table lines were tagged).
- Removing the non-body classes gives 0.966.

**2504.09409 (0.780): display math leaking into truth through macro-aliased environments, plus math fragments in extracted.**
- Counts: T-only 2,849, of which 2,086 is math residue. E-only 2,310: 547 math fragments, 379 figure text and 532 digit/char fragments.
- The source defines `\newcommand{\be}{\begin{equation}}` (and `\bea` for eqnarray).
  - latex_refs.rs `remove_environments` runs **before** `expand_macros`, so `\be\label{settings}\begin{gathered}…\ee` survives into truth: `"s satisfy K_s={108_s_s_s^2, 5C_s_s^2, 1L_f^2_s^2}, _s,k=K_s, >0, k=0,…,K_s-1. with =40(M_f^2+M_h^2+…"`.
  - It also produces `&`-aligned residue: `"that &_x ( x, ^k;) - _x ( y, ^k; ) _2 &f(x) - f(y) _2 + _i = 1^m ( c_i ( x ) +_i^k )…"`.
- Extracted keeps display-math fragments and an untagged algorithm box: `"Note that for any t = 1, . . . , K,\nwhere the inequality follows from Φ(x) ≥ Φinf, Lemma 4.2, |ci(xᵗ)| ≤ M̄c…"` and `"Algorithm 2 Multi-stage mirror descent linearized augmented Lagrangian method\nOutput: x̃T\n1: Set…"`.
- Variant (b) gives 0.947.

**2603.05575 (0.783): inline math everywhere.** This is a 123-page statistics paper, 25k words per side.
- Counts: T-only 5,558, of which 3,012 is math residue, 1,101 math-form spelling and 1,162 chars/digits. E-only 5,416: 940 math fragments, 881 spelling, 795 figure text, 1,236 char/digit/other fragments and a 174-word untagged algorithm.
- Examples:
  - Truth `"V(x_0)/|J_(x_0)| = V(x_0)/|J_(x_0)|(1 + o_p"` vs PDF `"θ̂(x0), η̂λ(x0; θ), Ĵλ(x0"`.
  - Truth `"Q_h(x_0;)^1/2 =w_x_0,h,_L^2"`.
  - Extracted `"…tuning constants cop, cloc, and c\nbias.\n1: Step 1: Tuning and RKHS localization weights\n2: Randomly partition {1, . . . , N}…"`, an algorithm without the algorithm role.
- 61 U+FFFD remain in the extracted text.
- Variant (a) gives 0.819, variant (b) 0.925.

## 3. Ceiling

I simulated each ceiling per paper by deleting the classified tokens from each side, recomputing the exact LCS, and taking the mean over 60 papers, the same way `align` is computed.

| Variant | What is removed | Dev mean | Recall | Precision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Now | — | **0.877** | 92.1% | 82.3% |
| **(a) strict** | E: figure, table, caption, math (runs ≥6), code, footnote, front, running heads, reference leakage, duplicates. T: detex noise, tokens not in the PDF, glued tokens | **0.933** | 92.3% | 94.0% |
| **(b)** | (a), plus both math-token sides: T math residue, T and E single-char/digit fragments, E math-glyph fragments | **0.967** | 97.0% | 96.4% |
| (c) | (b), plus truth-lost appendix and supplement treated as matched | 0.967 | 97.0% | 96.5% |
| (d) | (c), plus every extraction-side body error "fixed": reading order, spelling, over-tag, footnotes, appendix cut (tokens removed from both sides) | 0.985 | 99.5% | 97.7% |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2509.04183 | 0.982 | 0.987 | 0.993 |
| 2506.23487 | 0.774 | 0.968 | 0.987 |

What the variants show:
- **Non-body extracted text is the precision gap.** Removing it moves precision from 82% to 94%.
- **Math tokenization is the rest.** The mismatch between detexed LaTeX and PDF math glyphs is about 3.4 points on top of that.
- **Real lost body text is small.** Over-tag, footnotes, appendix and reading order together are about 1.8 points (d − c).

**Gains overlap and are not additive.** For example, E single-char/digit fragments count in G2 but are partly figure-label debris (G1). The realistic combined figure is (d) − base ≈ +0.108.

**Essentially solved:**
- Non-math spelling: 218 words.
- Broken hyphenation: 117 cases.
- Running heads: 766 words.
- Appendix after references: 573 words, down from 86k in loop 10.
- U+FFFD: 0 in 2108.04588.

**Caveats.**
- Over-tag counts are lower bounds; `tagged_lines` is capped in 17 papers.
- Class boundaries between "figure text" and "captions" are fuzzy where one run mixes labels and caption text (2509.24852).
- The removal gains in section 4 assume each fix is perfect.
